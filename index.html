HOW-TO GUIDE

How to Generate an Audio Overview in NotebookLM

Google NotebookLM features an innovative Deep Dive Audio Overview that creates a 2-host conversational podcast discussing your lecture materials.

  1. 1
    Open your Notebook in NotebookLM

    Navigate to your Operating Systems notebook containing your uploaded lecture PDFs.

  2. 2
    Locate the Audio Overview Studio

    In the upper right corner of the Notebook guide, locate the "Audio Overview" card.

  3. 3
    Click "Generate"

    NotebookLM will synthesize a 10 to 15-minute podcast between two AI hosts who explain complex algorithms, memory paging, and deadlock with lively analogies.

  4. 4
    Download the Generated Audio

    Click the 3 dots menu on the generated audio player and choose "Download" (.mp3 file).

  5. 5
    Load into this Player

    Select the downloaded .mp3 using the file picker on the left to listen with our custom controls and progress tracking.

CAPSTONE 41 SPECIFICATION

About the Project

Comprehensive CSE Capstone Project Documentation, Architecture, and Academic Guidelines.

Academic Year 2026
CSE CAPSTONE 41

Smart Notes and Study Guide Builder with NotebookLM

An educational technology software system engineered to streamline academic revision for undergraduate engineering students by bridging university lecture slides with Google NotebookLM's source-grounded intelligence.

HTML5 Vanilla CSS3 JavaScript (ES6+) Browser LocalStorage Google NotebookLM Web Audio API
🎯

Project Objective

Develop a modern, attractive, beginner-friendly web dashboard that empowers college students to convert heavy lecture slide decks and readings into exam study guides, summaries, viva FAQs, MCQ quizzes, timelines, and audio overviews through structured AI workflows.

🚧

Problem Statement

Undergraduate engineering students handle 5 to 6 heavy technical courses simultaneously, accumulating thousands of unstructured slides per semester. Manual revision note creation is tedious, while ungrounded public AI models suffer from severe factual hallucinations.

👥

Target Audience

Undergraduate Computer Science & Engineering college students, academic instructors, and self-paced learners seeking rapid, verified revision tools for technical curricula.

⚙

System Workflow

1. Local PDF management → 2. Source upload to Google NotebookLM → 3. Prompt execution using our optimized templates → 4. Local storage & formatting → 5. 8-Point Verification Audit & MCQ Testing.

🌟

Key Benefits

- Zero backend dependency for seamless local deployment.
- Complete user privacy with client-side localStorage.
- Source grounding through NotebookLM citations.
- Multimodal study through interactive quizzes and audio players.

⚠

Limitations & AI Ethics

- Direct NotebookLM API is not publicly open; hence this platform functions as a structured pipeline partner.
- Output accuracy is dependent on quality of source lecture slides.
- Mandates human verification before high-stakes exams.

🛡

Responsible AI Principles in Higher Education

Core standards adhered to throughout the development of Capstone 41:

1. Transparency

We do not fake AI responses or falsely simulate backend servers. NotebookLM is explicitly linked as an external Google tool.

2. Source Grounding

All AI study aids should trace back directly to lecture PDFs uploaded by the student with citation verification.

3. Human-in-the-Loop

The student retains primary cognitive authority through our built-in 8-point verification checklist and MCQ quizzes.

4. Data Privacy

Notes, quizzes, and verification audits are saved strictly in the user's browser localStorage without data harvesting.
